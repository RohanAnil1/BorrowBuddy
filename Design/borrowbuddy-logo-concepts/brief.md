# BorrowBuddy logo exploration

Requested source: https://github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill

BorrowBuddy lets verified university students lend and borrow useful items, with
clear pickup, return and accountability workflows. Its identity should feel
resourceful, dependable, social and direct. Students encounter the mark in the
website header, Android launcher, notifications and membership ID cards.

Assumptions: keep the name, existing navy/blue `#021249` / `#003993`, amber
`#cb840e`, paper `#f0f0f0`, uppercase typography and campus cargo character.
This is a logo exploration, not a change to the website's layouts or features.
The existing CargoMark provides visual equity: an angular folded form, square
silhouette and a firm shelf-like base. Keep those cues in the refresh option.
The user chooses the direction; production logo assets remain unchanged during
concept exploration.

## Research and word map

The skill's local library contains 23 education and 20 ecommerce-retail marks.
Education leans blue, while both categories commonly use combination marks,
containment and monoline construction. Study references: Patreon (reduction),
Pagekit (open negative space), Precursor (letter construction), Zendesk
(complementary geometry), Medium (proportion) and Tidal (modular repetition).
These are construction references, not shapes to copy or include in the app.

Avoid generic shopping bags, graduation caps, handshakes, shields/checkmarks,
recycling arrows, infinity links, stock-font BB initials and parcel cubes.
Word map: borrow -> temporary access -> outward/inward; buddy -> equal partners
-> paired forms; return -> continuity -> a counter-direction; campus cargo ->
checkout slip -> fold -> shelf. Build one defining gesture per mark.

## Construction

A: upright B on a 256-unit canvas, broad stem and two generous counters; opposing
counter tips imply lending and returning. Keep the letter readable before the
secondary idea is noticed. B: two equal opposing bracket modules, straight edges
and 45-degree outside cuts; the central opening belongs to both. C: an evolution
of the existing angular fold, with a 45-degree cut, open counter and stable base.
All begin as filled black geometry; no colour is needed to distinguish them.

The concept lockups outline the project's existing Noto Sans Mono font, whose
embedded licence is SIL Open Font License 1.1. They introduce no new font family.
The SVGs have no live text, embedded images, filters or strokes. Concept work
has not received trademark clearance and is not a final asset kit.

The requested skill's concept checkpoint applies: show three tested concepts and
recommend one, then wait for the chosen direction before building the final kit
or replacing website, Android and membership-card branding.
